About PathPredict

Empowering students to unlock their career potential using data-driven insights.

Our Vision

The Placement Prediction System is an intelligent assessment platform designed to bridge the gap between academic preparation and industry expectations. By analyzing key performance and capability indicators, the system predicts a student's recruitment potential and outlines clear, actionable improvement paths.

Core Objectives

  • Early Assessment: Help students evaluate their profile during their pre-final year.
  • Gap Identification: Pinpoint critical soft skills or technical languages that require attention.
  • Actionable Roadmap: Recommend direct actions rather than just scoring the profile.

How The Predictive Engine Works

A look at the underlying logical architecture of the placement system.

01

Data Intake

Captures student profiles including CGPA, programming languages, backlogs, and mock aptitude performance.

02

Feature Weighting

Calculates weighted metrics relative to historical placement datasets where CGPA, technical skills, and internships hold top priorities.

03

Inference Engine

Maps the student profile against industry standards to evaluate probability of getting placed in different corporate tiers.

04

Insights Generator

Dynamically structures personalized growth cards, highlights positive traits, and constructs specific feedback reports.
