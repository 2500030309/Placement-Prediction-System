Welcome to the Placement Prediction System

Your Smart Career Prediction Assistant

The Placement Prediction System is designed to help students estimate their placement opportunities based on their academic performance, technical skills, and other important factors.

This project uses Machine Learning techniques to analyze student data and predict the likelihood of getting placed in a company. It provides valuable insights that can help students improve their preparation for campus placements.

Through this application, users can enter their details, view prediction results, and understand the factors that influence placement outcomes. The goal is to guide students toward better career planning and skill development.

Project Features

• Student Information Entry
• Placement Prediction using Machine Learning
• Easy-to-Use Interface
• Fast and Accurate Results
• Career Guidance Support

We hope this system helps students gain confidence and prepare effectively for their future careers.
